# Privacy Policy for Daily Protein

**Effective date:** 16 October 2026

Daily Protein is an Android app by DevSyndicate for tracking how much protein
you eat. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Daily Protein does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything you
enter stays on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where

The app keeps the following **only on your device**, in the app's private
storage:

- The foods you add, with their protein, calories and price
- What you log each day, and your daily goals
- Your settings, such as theme and currency
- Photos you choose for your foods. The app keeps its own copy of each photo
  so that it still works if the original is deleted from your gallery.

None of this leaves your phone. The app has no account, no sign-in and no
server of its own.

## No internet access

Daily Protein does not request permission to use the internet, so it cannot
send your data anywhere. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking of any kind.

## Photos

When you add a photo to a food, Android's own photo picker or camera opens.
The app receives only the photo you choose, and does not get access to the rest
of your gallery.

## Backups

You can export your data to a file and import it again. The export is saved
only where you choose, using Android's file picker. What happens to that file
after that, such as copying it to cloud storage, is up to you. Photos are not
included in backups.

## App updates

If you installed Daily Protein from Google Play, the app may ask Google Play
whether a newer version is available, and Google Play may offer to download it.
This check is handled by Google Play, not by Daily Protein, and is covered by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time from **Settings → Clear All Data**.
Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your phone.

## Children

Daily Protein is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information
from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with
